# Community Code of Conduct

NETVERSE aims to be a constructive, respectful place for builders, researchers and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and act in good faith.
- Give specific, useful feedback.
- Protect privacy and never share credentials or personal data.
- Keep technical discussions focused on evidence and user benefit.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats or discriminatory language
- Spam, scams or misleading financial claims
- Publishing private keys, tokens, wallet seed phrases or security vulnerabilities
- Impersonation or attempts to gain unauthorized access

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and platform.
