# NETVERSE Closed Beta — Compute Contributors

NETVERSE is preparing a limited closed beta for people who want to contribute idle device compute to real, validated NETVERSE workloads.

## What this beta is for

The target service connects three real activities:

1. a NETVERSE customer uses a paid Premium or AI Agent capability;
2. an explicitly enabled contributor device performs an approved NETVERSE compute workload;
3. only completed and server-validated work becomes eligible for revenue allocation.

For shared resources used to power AI Agents and Premium plans, the canonical allocation is:

- **20%** of attributable revenue to the resource contributor;
- **80%** to NETVERSE.

Being online, sending heartbeats, or merely connecting a device does **not** earn a reward. Work must be completed and validated.

## Current beta boundary

This is an early-access contributor program, not an investment product and not a promise of income.

The production payment-to-compute accounting chain is still passing final security, build and real-device gates. Until those gates are complete, NETVERSE will not represent unverified activity as paid work and will not invent simulated earnings.

The first approved workload is a bounded deterministic SHA-256 batch task. It is designed to prove authenticated job delivery, local execution, signed results, independent server validation and exact accounting before broader workloads are enabled.

Arbitrary downloaded code is not supported.

## Who we want

We are looking for technically curious early contributors who:

- have an Android device they can dedicate to controlled testing;
- are comfortable testing pre-release software and reporting failures;
- understand that the first beta is intentionally narrow;
- can keep the device on Wi-Fi and, when required, charging;
- want to help validate a transparent resource-sharing model rather than speculate on future returns.

## Safety and consent

Compute sharing is opt-in. Device policy can require Wi-Fi, prohibit mobile data, require charging, and limit the configured CPU policy. NETVERSE uses paired device identity and signed requests/results for the closed-beta path.

Do not contribute a device you do not own or have permission to use.
