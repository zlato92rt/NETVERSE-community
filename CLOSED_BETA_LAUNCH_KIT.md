# NETVERSE Closed Beta Launch Kit

Use this page when sharing NETVERSE with technical early adopters. Keep claims aligned with the current public status.

## One-line description

NETVERSE is building a consent-based distributed compute network where paid AI/Premium usage can be connected to real, independently validated work performed by contributor devices.

## Contributor economics

For shared resources used to power AI Agents and Premium plans:

- 20% of attributable revenue goes to the resource contributor.
- 80% goes to NETVERSE.
- No completed and validated work means no contributor allocation.
- Being online or sending a heartbeat is not paid work.

This is usage-based revenue sharing, not an investment product and not a promise of income.

## What exists in the closed-beta engineering path

- cryptographically paired Android device identity;
- signed, replay-resistant job fetch and result submission;
- explicit compute-sharing consent and network/charging policy;
- bounded deterministic SHA-256 batch workload;
- independent server-side result validation;
- exact integer accounting rules for the 20/80 allocation;
- negative/recovery and real-device E2E acceptance checklist.

## What is not yet a public production claim

The payment-to-compute production chain is still gated on deployment/runtime validation, transactional monetary guarantees and physical-device E2E evidence. Do not describe beta queue participation as earning money until those gates are complete.

## Suggested community post

**Title:** Building a consent-based distributed compute network for paid AI usage — looking for closed-beta Android contributors

NETVERSE is testing a resource-sharing model where an opted-in contributor device performs a bounded compute job for paid AI/Premium usage. Jobs are delivered to a cryptographically paired device, results are signed, and the server independently validates the work before it can be accounted.

Our canonical split for attributable revenue from shared resources is 20% to the contributor and 80% to NETVERSE. Online status alone earns nothing; only completed and validated work is eligible.

The first workload is intentionally narrow: deterministic SHA-256 batches on Android. We are using it to validate identity, consent, delivery, execution, anti-replay, independent validation and accounting before broader workloads.

We are recruiting technical Android testers now. This is a closed beta, not an investment or guaranteed-income offer, and we will not label unverified activity as paid work.

Technical/public status and signup are in the NETVERSE community hub.
