# NETVERSE Press & Media Kit

## One-line description

**NETVERSE is building a Web + Android platform that combines AI agents, creator commerce and verified consent-based distributed compute.**

## Short description

NETVERSE is a pre-launch technology platform connecting paid AI services, a creator marketplace and opted-in device compute through one authenticated economic layer. Users can access AI agents and digital products, creators can publish reviewed products, and participating Android devices can execute bounded workloads that are independently validated before contributor revenue attribution is recorded.

## Boilerplate

NETVERSE is being built from Bulgaria with global ambitions. The platform combines AI-agent services, Premium access, a creator marketplace, verified payments, authenticated support/community systems and consent-based distributed compute across Web and Android. NETVERSE is currently in advanced development and is not represented as fully public or production-ready. Public project materials intentionally avoid fabricated traction and keep credentials, payment security and core infrastructure private.

## Core themes for coverage

### AI agents becoming paid products
NETVERSE is building reviewed/versioned AI-agent publishing with authoritative paid access rather than treating agents only as demos.

### Creator commerce + AI
BuilderZone is designed to let creators publish digital tools and products with explicit ownership and marketplace settlement.

### Verified distributed compute
NETVERSE's Android compute foundation is consent-based and intentionally bounded while device identity, job delivery, execution, validation and accounting are proven end-to-end.

### Economic integrity
The project is designed so uptime alone is not treated as paid work and economic events have server-side audit/integrity rules.

### Building from Bulgaria for a global market
NETVERSE is being developed in Bulgaria while targeting a global community of users, creators, builders and compute contributors.

## Product economics currently under development

- Distributed compute: **20% contributor / 80% NETVERSE** of attributable revenue after validated work.
- BuilderZone: **80% builder / 20% NETVERSE**.

These are product economics, not an investment return promise or guaranteed contributor income.

## Current stage

- Advanced development / pre-launch.
- Public launch waitlist open.
- Early-customer queue open.
- Android compute contributor closed-beta queue open.
- Core production backend and security-sensitive infrastructure remain private.

## What NETVERSE does not claim

NETVERSE does not present:
- simulated transactions as revenue;
- waitlist members as customers;
- uptime/heartbeats as paid compute work;
- community donations as investments;
- product economics as guaranteed returns.
