# NETVERSE — Public Investor & Partner Brief

> **Stage:** advanced development / pre-launch  
> **Focus:** AI agents, creator commerce, verified payments, consent-based distributed compute, Web + Android  
> **Region:** built from Bulgaria with global ambitions

## The idea

NETVERSE is building a multi-product platform where users can discover and pay for AI services, creators can publish digital products and AI agents, and opted-in devices can contribute bounded compute work that is independently validated before any contributor revenue attribution is recorded.

The long-term thesis is that **AI services, creator distribution and edge compute should not live in isolated products**. NETVERSE is building one authenticated economic layer across them.

## What is being built

- **AI Agents** — published, reviewed and versioned AI services with verified paid access.
- **Premium** — subscription-style paid access backed by authoritative payment verification.
- **BuilderZone** — creator marketplace with buyer ownership and settlement logic.
- **Distributed Compute** — consent-based Android compute with signed delivery and server validation.
- **Contributor Economics** — attributable compute revenue is split only after real work is validated.
- **Support + Community** — authenticated support tickets, moderated community chat and notifications.
- **Web + Android** — one product ecosystem across browser and mobile.

## What is different

### 1. Payments are tied to real access
NETVERSE is being built around verified payment → entitlement → service consumption, rather than simulated purchases or demo revenue.

### 2. Compute is not paid for being online
Heartbeat/uptime alone does not earn revenue. Contributor allocation is intended only for completed and independently validated work.

### 3. Publisher and marketplace state is reviewed
AI-agent versions and creator-marketplace assets use explicit lifecycle and audit rules rather than uncontrolled public publishing.

### 4. Economic events are auditable
Premium usage, marketplace settlement, paid-agent accounting and verified donation acceptance are designed around durable server-side records and integrity checks.

### 5. Security-sensitive infrastructure stays private
The public repository is deliberately limited to community, documentation, product concepts and launch communication.

## Current product direction

The first controlled compute workload is intentionally bounded and deterministic while device identity, consent, job delivery, local execution, validation and accounting are proven end-to-end.

Arbitrary downloaded-code execution on contributor devices is **not** part of the current Android design.

## Business model

Potential revenue channels include:

- Premium access;
- paid AI-agent usage;
- BuilderZone marketplace fees;
- NETVERSE share of attributable distributed-compute revenue;
- future enterprise / infrastructure services.

Current canonical economics under development:

- **Distributed compute:** contributor 20% / NETVERSE 80% of attributable revenue after validated work.
- **BuilderZone:** builder 80% / NETVERSE 20%.

These are product economics, not an investment return promise.

## Why now

AI-agent products are moving from demos toward paid workflows, while AI infrastructure and compute efficiency remain major areas of investment. NETVERSE is being built at the intersection of AI services, marketplaces and distributed infrastructure.
