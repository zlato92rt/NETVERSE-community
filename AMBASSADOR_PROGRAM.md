# NETVERSE Global Ambassador & Translation Program

NETVERSE is building an international community before public launch.

This program is for people who want to help introduce NETVERSE to their local developer, startup, AI, creator or technology communities.

## Who can participate

- developers;
- students;
- AI creators;
- startup community members;
- technical writers;
- translators;
- content creators;
- Discord/Telegram community organizers;
- open-source contributors.

No follower minimum is required.

## Activities

Choose one or more:
- translate public NETVERSE materials;
- share verified milestone updates in your language;
- introduce NETVERSE to local AI/startup/developer communities;
- organize an online community Q&A;
- recruit early testers or builders;
- collect structured feedback;
- write a technical explainer;
- create a short educational video;
- help moderate public community spaces.

## Rules

Ambassadors must not:
- promise investment returns;
- claim guaranteed contributor income;
- claim partnerships that do not exist;
- present waitlist members as customers;
- present demo/simulated transactions as revenue;
- impersonate NETVERSE staff;
- request private keys, seed phrases, passwords or financial secrets;
- spam unrelated communities.

Every ambassador should link back to the public NETVERSE hub so claims can be checked.

## Recognition

Before any formal reward program exists, participation is recognition-based.

Possible recognition:
- contributor credit in the public community repository;
- ambassador profile in a future community page;
- early access consideration;
- invitation to community calls;
- feedback priority.

No financial compensation, token allocation or future ownership should be assumed unless separately and explicitly agreed in writing.

## How to join

Use the public GitHub ambassador issue and include:
- country/region;
- languages;
- technical/community background;
- platforms or communities you know;
- which activities you want to help with.

Do not post private personal information.

## Local launch squads

When enough volunteers exist in one language/region, create a small launch squad with:
- one community coordinator;
- one translator/editor;
- one technical reviewer;
- one content/community contributor.

The goal is not to copy-paste global marketing. Each local group should adapt NETVERSE to its community while keeping technical and economic claims accurate.
